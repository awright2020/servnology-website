The problem

The phone rings. The guest waits.

Every hotelier knows the moment. A guest has just arrived, and the line lights up again.

One desk. Two guests. Only one gets your attention.

Your agent has to choose between the person on the phone and the person at the desk. Neither gets the welcome you trained for.

Multiply that by every shift, and the phone quietly becomes the most demanding guest in the building.

What hotels are up against

Three problems, one answer.

The desk can't be covered every hour.

Many hotels can't afford to staff the front desk every moment of the day. Overnight, during breaks and in the middle of the check-in rush, calls go unanswered and bookings go elsewhere.

With Servnology, someone always picks up, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, without adding a shift to the schedule.

Routine calls eat up the shift.

So many calls are the same handful of questions: breakfast hours, the Wi-Fi password, parking, directions. Each one is simple. Together they pull your team away from the lobby again and again.

The voice agent takes these repetitive calls, so your staff can spend their time on work that needs a person.

The hard calls need a calm voice.

The calls that matter most often come at the worst moment: a guest complaint, a maintenance problem, a special request.

The voice agent handles those too. It listens, responds with care, and logs or routes the issue so your team can follow up with everything they need.
