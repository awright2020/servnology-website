    How it works

    Four steps, no new phone number.

    1. 1

      A guest calls

      Your hotel's existing number rings, just as it does today.

    2. 2

      The agent answers

      A natural, friendly voice picks up right away, every time.

    3. 3

      It handles or hands off

      Questions, requests and complaints are handled on the call. Anything that needs a person goes to your staff with context.

    4. 4

      Your desk stays with guests

      Your team keeps its attention on the people in the lobby.

    What it does

    Everything a great front desk call should be.

    Available 24/7
    Overnight, on holidays and during the check-in rush. No call goes to voicemail, even when no one is at the desk.
    Natural conversation
    Guests speak normally and get clear, gracious answers in a voice that suits your property.
    Handles any guest call
    Questions about rates, parking, breakfast and directions, plus requests and guest complaints.
    Smart handoff to staff
    When a call needs a human, it reaches the right person, along with what the guest already said.
    Works with your systems
